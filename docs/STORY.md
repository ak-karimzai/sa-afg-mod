# Story – "The Valley"

**Farid Noori** used to drive a taxi. After eight years working in Iran he comes home to **Qala-e-Naw**, a market town
on the Shomali plain. The valley he remembers is now squeezed by the **Black Stone** (*Sang-e-Siah*), a smuggling gang
that extorts the bazaar, robs the aid trucks and holds the roads into the Ghorband valley. Its leader, **Siahdast**
("Black Hand"), runs everything from an old mountain fortress.

All people, groups and events are fictional. The terrain is real, but the towns and their names are invented.

## Missions

Every mission begins with a cutscene at the contact and ends with a scene at the destination. Objectives are shown
with radar blips and red checkpoints; timers, damage bars and enemy counters appear at the top right of the screen.

| # | Mission | Contact | What happens | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Homecoming** | Rahim | Take his old pickup and deliver flour to the baker in Deh-e-Bagh. Half way, a Black Stone "road tax" post demands money and opens fire if you do not stop. Keep the pickup in one piece. | $300 |
| 2 | **Market Day** | Rahim | Four Black Stone men are shaking down a stall keeper. Take your father's pistol and drive them out of the bazaar while the villagers run for cover. They drop their "tax" money. | $500 |
| 3 | **Medicine for Shahrak** | Rahim | Race the medicine crate across the plain to the Shahrak clinic in 3 minutes. The doctor runs out to meet you, and Captain Nasir phones afterwards. | $800 |
| 4 | **Darwaza** | Nasir | At night, help three policemen hold Darwaza Checkpoint against three waves. The first comes on foot, the next two arrive by pickup with men on the slopes; the last has eight attackers and an RPG gunner. If all three policemen die, the checkpoint falls. You keep the AK-47. | $1,500 |
| 5 | **Burj-e-Khak** | Nasir | The doctor has been kidnapped. Night raid on the ruins east of the base: eight guards on their rounds and two marksmen on the perimeter. Free the doctor, get him into a vehicle and drive him to Shahrak while Black Stone pickups waiting on the road ram you and shoot from the passenger seat. | $2,500 |
| 6 | **Aid Convoy** | Hamid | Drive the aid truck from Camp Sangar up the Ghorband valley with Karim and Jawed following in an army jeep. At the ambush an RPG hits the road, a bandit pickup blocks it and the soldiers dismount to cover you. Deliver to the elder of Dara-e-Sabz. | $2,000 |
| 7 | **Fuel and Fire** | Hamid | The Black Stone fuel dump in occupied Sar-e-Kotal. Come up at dusk, plant charges on three fuel stacks (or shoot the barrels), survive the alarm and the reinforcements, get clear before the 30-second timer runs out. | $3,000 |
| 8 | **Black Stone** | Nasir | Raid the bandit camp at Sang-e-Siah at first light. Ten guards walk their rounds until one of them notices you; then a pickup brings two more up from the valley. Clear the camp and recover the stolen aid. | $3,000 |
| 9 | **The Pass** | Hamid | Three trucks and two escort pickups climb the mountain road to the fortress at night. From the ambush position above the switchbacks, destroy all three trucks with the RPG before they get past you. The escort comes for you once the first truck burns. | $4,000 |
| 10 | **Eagle's Nest** | Hamid | Fly a helicopter to Qala-e-Kohna and circle it until the photos are done, while an RPG team fires at you. Land back on the helipad. | $2,500 |
| 11 | **Qala-e-Kohna** | Hamid | Before dawn, lead four soldiers up to the fortress gate. The gate is blown and you storm the fortress (14 defenders, two RPG teams). When his guard is nearly gone Siahdast runs for a pickup and flees down the mountain road: take the squad's jeep and stop him before he reaches the valley. | $10,000 |
| 12 | **Vulture** | Hamid | The Black Stone remnants flee down the Ghorband valley in five pickups. Take the Hunter and destroy every one of them before they cross Darwaza. Epilogue: tea with Rahim at sunrise. | $15,000 |

After the last mission the valley is free to explore. A **Hunter** helicopter and a **Rhino** tank appear at Camp Sangar.
