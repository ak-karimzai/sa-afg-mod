# Story – "The Valley"

**Farid Noori** used to drive a taxi. After eight years working in Iran he comes home to **Qala-e-Naw**, a market town
on the Shomali plain. The valley he remembers is now squeezed by the **Black Stone** (*Sang-e-Siah*), a smuggling gang
that extorts the bazaar, robs the aid trucks and holds the roads into the Ghorband valley. Its leader, **Siahdast**
("Black Hand"), runs everything from an old mountain fortress.

All people, groups and events are fictional. The terrain is real, but the towns and their names are invented.

## Act two: The Snow Road

Three weeks after Siahdast's death the man who paid for the Black Stone comes over the northern pass:
**Haji Gul Zarghun**, an opium baron with a column of "Northerners". He wants the valley back, and he has a friend
inside it.
