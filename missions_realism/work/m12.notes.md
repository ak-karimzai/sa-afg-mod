# m12 Ashes notes
States (36@): 1 defend bazaar vs 10 raiders (37@..46@, 58@ alive count -> $426), 2 chase leader car (66@, waypoints $936/$960/$984, fail if it reaches end), 3 NEW aftermath.
## Hangs found / fixed
- Bike riders (idx 4,5) were never re-tasked after leaving bikes; they could idle far away so kill-all never hit 0. Fixed: unified re-task loop covers all 10.
- Convoy TASK_CAR_MISSION with no target could stall cars; riders in cars only left after 4 cycles. Now re-tasked, plus 2-minute fallback: any survivor >110m away is released (blip removed, handle -1) and not counted. Count treats handle -1 / dead / nonexistent as cleared.
- Phase 2: car stuck on a waypoint forever (no progress ~50 s and >80m from player) is hopped to its waypoint.
- Phase 1 -> 2 was instant; now aftermath state 3 always ends (24 s... max 40 s timeout).
## Behaviour changes
- Raiders: marksman (country rifle), shotgunner, armoured rifleman, light bikers; varied accuracy/health; drops enabled with limited ammo (150 AK, 200 uzi, 90 AK).
- Only 2 foot raiders engage at once; convoy launches on first player shot, first corpse, or after ~9 s, with radio line. Holdouts then join. Low health (<40) raiders flee 5 s per cycle.
- Aftermath: "area clear" text, ~6 s later a wounded straggler (pistol) appears from the far side; then leader car revealed (original AFA_09 flow). Leader and gunner armoured, drop weapons.
