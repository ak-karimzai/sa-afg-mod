# m10 Qala-e-Kohna
Hangs: (1) no player-dead check inside the main loop (only after the mission body returned), so death never ended the mission; added IS_PLAYER_PLAYING check in main and aftermath loops. (2) Boss car destroyed or boss out of car: loop returned early forever, boss idle on foot; now he attacks on foot (Lnew_bossfoot). (3) Boss only fled when fewer than 5 guards alive; unreachable guards could block it; added ~2 min fallback. (4) Boss death ended the mission instantly regardless of garrison; now a clear-and-verify phase with 60 s timeout fallback. Kill count uses IS_CHAR_DEAD (true for invalid handles), so deleted handles count as down.
Changes: guards have roles (4 shotgunners, 2 marksmen on walls, pistol officer 160hp, 2 armoured, 2 RPG as before), limited ammo and weapon drops; alarm by first corpse or shots near boss; assault splits rushers and flankers; wounded guards flee 6 s then return; 4 reinforcements refill dead slots when under 9 alive; ally-down shout; aftermath objective then allies' radio line then original success path.
New keys: X10A01..X10A05 in text_m10.fxt.
Risks: TASK_GOTO_CHAR_AIMING and array-element outputs in CREATE_CHAR untested in game; reinforcement spawn points reuse original spawn coords (may pop in view); bytes 5464.
Review fixes: (a) boss-breakout fallback timer 91@ now counts only while 36@=1, boss not yet fled and 4 or fewer guards alive; it resets to 0 whenever 5+ are alive, so a long fight no longer forces the boss to flee. (b) Approach triggers tightened (checkpoint 30 -> 18 m, boss-distance 75 -> 40 m) because the spawn is 63 m from the boss and the old trigger fired on the first tick; now the alarm (first corpse / shot within 70 m of boss) can actually fire first. Both paths retraced: 71@ reaching 0 still opens the flee gate; 91@ only resets, no new loops. check.py ok, 5485 bytes.

Crash fix: the morale check "100@[69@] > 1" used 001B (number > lvar) with the operands swapped, so the literal 1 was dereferenced as a variable (null read at gta_sa.exe+0x66298) the first time a wounded guard fled. Now 0019 IS_INT_LVAR_GREATER_THAN_NUMBER.
