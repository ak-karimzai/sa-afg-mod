# m21 Iron Harvest notes

## Hangs found
1. Roadblock ambush clear required the Bobcat (422) to be destroyed as well as all 4 men. Killing all men left the stage open forever (probable "killed all, nothing continues"). FIXED: car no longer required; if it still stands after the men die a 10 s grace runs, then the roadblock counts as broken. Also a 2 min hard timeout per roadblock.
2. Camp stage needed garrison dead AND ammo dump damaged, with no prompt after the last kill. FIXED: hint X21A02 when garrison is dead; after 60 s the dump counts as hit (fallback).
3. Camp enemies (incl. unreachable/stuck) had no fallback. FIXED: after 5 min (137@ > 1200 ticks) survivors are released; fleeing wounded men 70 m away are released.
4. Original IS_CHAR_DEAD on 83@/84@ relied on -1 handle behaviour; now explicit -1 checks.
5. Success was instant on last kill. FIXED: aftermath (last truck, verify text) before original success path.
Other states (enter Rhino, drive, fail on Rhino destroyed, death/arrest) judged fine.

## Behaviour
- Roadblock men: 120 rounds, drop weapons, advance in staggered aiming line; point man rushes, one armoured shotgunner, 2 RPG (4 rounds).
- Camp: roles (AK, 2 shotguns, marksman rifle, RPGs with 4 rounds, 2 armoured). Squad 1 engages at once; squad 2 holds until alert (first corpse, Rhino within 110 m, or 20 s) then flanks; marksman keeps 35-60 m.
- Reinforcement truck now spawns on alert (not at start), crew dismounts after ~14 s and fights on foot.
- Wounded (<30 hp) flee. Weapons dropped, limited ammo.
- Aftermath: after garrison + dump are down, a last truck (2 gunners) arrives (50 s cap), then "camp quiet" text and original success path.
