- Roles: AK riflemen, shotgunner, marksman (slot 3, holds high ground), pistol officer, armoured sentry; varied accuracy; limited ammo drops.
- Sentry that sees a dead comrade raises the noise meter; wounded sentries (<38 hp) flee after the alarm.
- Alarm: all sentries manoeuvre onto player (timed kill task); pair 1 reacts at once, pair 2 (shotgunner) arrives ~7 s later with radio line.
- Aftermath: last sentry no longer ends the stage on the same frame. State 4 "sweep" (>=5 s); after a firefight a wounded sentry crawls for the radio and must be finished (40 s timeout); then original charge objective.
- First charge planted: relief guard of 3 converges on the wall (second contact, does not block progress).
