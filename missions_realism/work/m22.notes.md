# m22 Traitor's Road - notes

## Hangs found
- Main chase loop never checked HAS_DEATHARREST_BEEN_EXECUTED: after player death/bust the script kept running until the convoy reached the fortress. Fixed (RETURN to the top-level fail check).
- Lead car wedged on terrain: only a re-task every ~4 s, no progress fallback. Fixed: after 40 s without waypoint progress the waypoint is skipped and the car re-tasked.
- Waypoint index could run past the 56-entry arrays ($934 unknown): clamped to 55 in the drive task subroutine.
- Success test (lead car dead OR Nasir dead) is sound, no hang. It also used to pass instantly after 3 s with the escort untouched.
- Ambush car spawns ahead of the convoy and can block it; covered by the stall skip.

## Behaviour changes
- Mixed gunners (AK/MP5/Uzi), varied accuracy, Nasir and his bodyguard wear armour, gunners drop limited ammo.
- Escort cars provide cover; when wrecked (health < 450), driver dead, or Nasir dead, crews bail out and fight on foot (officer rushes, gunner holds range aiming).
- First hits on Nasir's car (health < 1800): he panics, speed 17 -> 23, ambush called early, radio line.
- Ambush: rifleman now advances aiming at mid range, RPG man (3 rockets), plus a rifle marksman (33) on the shoulder; block car is cover.
- Aftermath: when Nasir falls his bodyguard flees, remaining escort fight; objective "finish off the escort"; counts ped alive only if exists, not dead, within 120 m; wounded (<35 hp) flee; 45 s hard timeout; then radio line and the original success path.
